# Kin Shipaton demo video script

Target length: **1 minute 50 seconds**  
Format: **1920×1080 landscape, 30 fps**  
Delivery: natural and conversational; aim for roughly 135–145 words per minute

## Recording setup

- Record one continuous landscape talking-head clip.
- Keep the camera at eye level and leave space around your shoulders for cropping.
- Use a quiet room and the clearest microphone available.
- Do not add music; the final edit will use only your voice and original Kin visuals.
- Hold still and stay silent for two seconds at the beginning and end.
- If a sentence goes wrong, pause, repeat the whole sentence, and continue. The final edit can remove the mistake.
- Upload the original `.mp4` or `.mov` file without compressing it through a messaging app.

## Word-for-word narration

### 0:00–0:08 — Opening, full face

Hi, I’m Kasey, an NUS student, and I built Kin. Messaging apps help us talk, but the moments that matter eventually disappear inside years of scrolling.

### 0:08–0:22 — Chats and the Kin Space

Kin is a private messenger designed to remember relationships. Every person gets a Kin Space that still feels familiar, but can have its own nickname, accent, wallpaper, shared media, and relationship sticker.

### 0:22–0:43 — Remember this

Here’s the main interaction. I open a meaningful message, choose Remember this, and decide whether it becomes a Moment, an important date, or a plan. I’ll save this one as Our first date. Kin keeps the original message attached, so the memory never loses its context.

### 0:43–0:59 — Relationship view and timeline

That Moment now appears in the relationship view, on the timeline, and in On This Day. Instead of searching through thousands of messages, the history of this relationship has somewhere intentional to live.

### 0:59–1:12 — Personalization

Each Space can also feel different. I can preview themes like Moonlit and change the wallpaper, without turning a relationship into a public profile, score, or surveillance tool.

### 1:12–1:31 — RevenueCat and Kin+

RevenueCat powers Kin Plus. Ordinary messaging and every Moment already saved stay free. Kin Plus unlocks premium relationship themes and unlimited new Moments. I tested purchasing and restoring Kin Plus through RevenueCat Test Store on Android, while Supabase keeps the entitlement and free limits server-authoritative.

### 1:31–1:44 — Build and verification

Kin is built with Expo, Supabase, and RevenueCat. Codex helped me turn the product requirements into implementation, test the privacy boundaries, and verify the release across three hundred and forty app tests, thirty-six backend tests, and fourteen end-to-end journeys.

### 1:44–1:50 — Closing, full face

Most messengers store messages. Kin remembers relationships.
